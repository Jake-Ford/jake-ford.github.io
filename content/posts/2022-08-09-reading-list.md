Less delicious than the food recommendations, but more so for the soul. My recent 2022 reading and listenings — yes, I am a recent audiobook convert.

Two I would highly recommend: the biography of James A. Baker III, which reads like a podcast version of the West Wing; and, sticking to the theme, Ron Silver aka Bruno Gianelli narrating Philip Roth's masterpiece, *The Plot Against America*.

Other highlights: fanboying over Olga Tokarczuk. I have a tendency of reading multiple works by the same author — finally got to *The Echo Maker* after loving *The Overstory*; did not disappoint but did not surpass. And I've gone through five or so Philip Roth pieces, starting with the unforgettable *Portnoy's Complaint*. But *Plot Against America* was stunning.

**October 2022 Update:** My best friend recommended a few mangas. I've put my nose up long enough — dove into *Solo Leveling*, absolutely gripping. Some 200 chapters later, no regrets.
