title: "Data Science Resources"
---

A cultivated list of resources I used to get started and stay current on data science topics — mainly for R and Python users, with some stats and CS fundamentals. It worked for me; your mileage may vary based on background.

## Courses

- [Intro to Data Science in Python](https://www.coursera.org/learn/python-data-analysis) — Coursera
- [Intro to Data Science in R](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/data-science-foundations-r) — Coursera
- [R Programming](https://www.coursera.org/learn/r-programming) — Coursera
- [Data Science Toolkit](https://www.coursera.org/learn/data-scientists-tools) — theory + some application
- [Chicago DS MLOps](https://chicagodatascience.github.io/MLOps/lecture3/)

## Books

- **[ISLR](https://www.statlearning.com/)** — the bible. Free, available for Python or R. If you read one book, read this.
- **[Python Machine Learning](https://www.amazon.com/Python-Machine-Learning-scikit-learn-TensorFlow/dp/1789955750/)** — Sebastian Raschka. The one I still keep at my desk.
- **[Python Data Science Handbook](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/)** — VanderPlas. Free online, great for getting up to speed on pandas/numpy.
- **[Advanced R](http://adv-r.had.co.nz/Introduction.html)** — Hadley Wickham. The recommended reading list in the intro alone is worth it.
- **[Build a Career in Data Science](https://www.amazon.com/Build-Career-Science-Jacqueline-Nolis/dp/1617296244)** — Nolis & Thompson. Great on the industry/career side.
- **[Machine Learning Yearning](https://nessie.ilab.sztaki.hu/~kornai/2020/AdvancedMachineLearning/Ng_MachineLearningYearning.pdf)** — Andrew Ng. Free PDF.
- **[Lantz ML in R](https://www.amazon.ca/Machine-Learning-techniques-predictive-modeling/dp/1784393908)** — super approachable intro to ML concepts with R code.
- [Large repo of free DS PDFs](https://github.com/LTS-tech-community/data-science-resources) — assembled by Jimmy Oty and Lawrence Juma.

## Job Boards

- **[Tech Jobs For Good](https://techjobsforgood.com/)** — mission-based tech jobs, full range of seniority.
- **[Ben Green's Job List](https://www.benzevgreen.com/jobs/)** — data science in social good sectors.
- aijobs.net, datajobs, outerjoin — internship and remote-focused.

## Blogs & Other

- **[Towards Data Science](https://towardsdatascience.com/)** — daily emails of digestible technical content. Probably my most-used resource.
- **[Anna-Lena Popkes](https://alpopkes.com/#projects)** — excellent Python walkthroughs of ML concepts.
- **[Youyang Gu](https://youyanggu.com/)** — did extraordinary COVID modeling during the pandemic. Excellent writer.
- **[Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/)** — not DS-specific, but Tyler Cowen is one of the most interesting thinkers writing publicly. His podcast is also great.
- **[CV Done Wrong](https://github.com/mottalrd/cross-validation-done-wrong/blob/master/Cross%20Validation%20done%20wrong.ipynb)** — quick read on cross-validation. Good refresher.
